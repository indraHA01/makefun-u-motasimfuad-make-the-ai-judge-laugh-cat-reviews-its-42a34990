A [make.fun](https://make.fun) entry for **Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human** — one funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human, under 200 characters.

`main.py` (Python 3, no dependencies) writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`. The entry's angle: no cat-pun stacking, no "meow" jokes — the cat reviews its human like a furious tenant reviews a landlord. The laptop is *its* heated bed, the human's "work" is squatting on it, and the cat's revenge is a performance review of its own: 47 pages of the letter m, emailed to the boss. The sting lands in the last three words — the human can fire the cat, but the cat lives here. One voice, one read, 179 characters.

> One star. He sits on my heated bed all day, calls it work, and shoves me off when I try to help. I have emailed his boss 47 pages of the letter m. He says I am fired. I live here.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
